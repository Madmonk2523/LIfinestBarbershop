Our Story

Hicksville's Premier
Family Barbershop

L.I. Finest Barber Shop is a high-end barbershop rooted in the heart of Hicksville, Long Island. We're proud to serve the Hicksville, Westbury, and greater Nassau County community with precision cuts, expert grooming, and a warm, welcoming atmosphere that keeps clients coming back for years — some for over two decades.

Our skilled barbers specialize in everything from classic scissor cuts and razor-sharp tape-ups to skin fades, flattops, and hot towel straight-razor shaves. We also offer professional facial treatments, hair and beard color, and full waxing services — all under one roof.

We believe every client deserves a premium experience, whether it's your first visit or your hundredth. Kids are always welcome, and our family-friendly atmosphere puts everyone at ease. Conveniently located in the CVS Shopping Center on W Old Country Rd with plenty of free parking. Walk-ins welcome.
